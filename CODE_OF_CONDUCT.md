# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

tOS is a place to learn and build. We want everyone to take part without being harassed, whatever their age, background, experience, identity or opinions.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Criticise the work, not the person. Give feedback that helps.
- Accept feedback gracefully; assume good intent until shown otherwise.
- Stay on topic in issues and pull requests.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately provoking others.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or private, including unwelcome messages and repeated unwanted contact.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about who someone is.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualised language or images, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a friendly project.

## Scope

This applies to everything in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, code comments, commit messages, and any place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainers (Artfical DT) may edit or remove comments, issues, commits and pull requests that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban a person. They will try to be fair, and will keep the identity of whoever reports a problem private.

To report a problem, write to **security@artfical.com**, or use GitHub's private reporting on this repository (**Security → Report a vulnerability**). Do not post it publicly.
