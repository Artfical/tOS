## The top bar

The bar along the top of the screen shows, from the left: the system menu, then the name of the app you are working in with its menus (for example **Desktop**, **File**, **Window**), and on the right the status icons, the search icon and the **clock** with the date. Click the clock to open the **Notification Centre** (see [Notifications](/tos/notifications)).

The menus change with the app you are in.

## The dock

The dock at the bottom holds the apps you use most: the **Launcher**, **Files**, **Terminal**, **tFire**, **Notepad** and others, ending with the **Trash**. A small dot under an app means it is running. Click an app to start it or to bring it to the front.

![The dock, with dots under the running apps](shot:dock_indicators.png)

- **Magnification:** icons grow when the pointer passes over them. Turn it off in Settings, Desktop & Dock.
- **Open-app indicators:** the dots can be turned off in the same place.
- **Hover:** point at an icon to see its name.

## ![](icon:launcher) The Launcher

The first icon in the dock opens the **Launcher**: every app in one grid. Click an app to open it. Click anywhere else to close the Launcher.

![The Launcher](shot:launcher.png)

## Windows

Every window has a title bar with three round buttons at the top left (close, minimise, zoom) and the title in the middle.

| To do this | Do this |
|---|---|
| Move a window | Drag its title bar |
| Resize a window | Drag the bottom-right corner |
| Bring a window to the front | Click it |
| Minimise a window | Click the minimise button. It shrinks into the dock |
| Close a window | Click the close button |

![A window above the dock](shot:window_above_dock.png)

## The desktop itself

The desktop shows the wallpaper and the files you drop there. **Right-click** an empty part of the desktop for a menu:

- **New Folder** and **New File** (a new file opens straight in Notepad)
- **Open Files** and **Open Terminal**
- **About This Computer**
- **Change Background**
- **Refresh Desktop**

Change the wallpaper and the dock in **Settings**, Desktop & Dock: the choices are *Dusk*, *Ocean*, *Graphite* and a *Picture* of your own. See [Settings](/tos/settings).

## Dragging and dropping

Files can be dragged between apps. Drag a file out of a **Files** window onto the desktop to leave it there, onto an app window to open it there (a text file onto Notepad, a picture onto the Image Viewer, a page onto tFire), or onto the **Trash** to delete it.

## ![](icon:trash) The Trash

Files you delete from Files go to the **Trash** at the end of the dock. The Trash is a folder you can open: click it to see what is in it, and **Empty Trash** to delete it all for good.

![A file dragged onto the Trash](shot:drag_trash.png)

## Keyboard

Most things can be done from the keyboard too. In Files, the arrow keys move between files and Shift extends the selection; in Notepad there are shortcuts for Find, Replace and Go to line (see [Notepad, Paint and viewers](/tos/productivity)). In the Terminal you type commands. **Ctrl+C** stops a program that is running in the Terminal.

## Sound

tOS plays short sounds when you open or close a window and when you click a menu or a dock icon. Turn them off with `beep off`. See [Audio](/tos/audio).

## Changing the screen size

The desktop starts at 1280x720. Settings, Displays lists the sizes your display supports: 1024x768, 1280x720, 1366x768, 1600x900 and 1920x1080.
