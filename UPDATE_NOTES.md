# What's new in tOS

## desktop
- **Search** — Ctrl+Space (or the top-right corner): find apps, Settings pages and your files.
- **Spaces** — Ctrl+Left / Ctrl+Right slide between desktops; each can have its own wallpaper.
- **Mission Control** — Ctrl+Up or F3: every window at a glance; click, or drag a window to another Space.
- **Alt+Tab** — a picture of every window; let go of Alt to switch.
- **Snap** — drag a window to an edge for half the screen, to the top for all of it.
- **Pin** — right-click a title bar to keep a window on top.
- The top-bar speaker mutes the sounds; the Notification Centre has Dark/Light and Mute tiles.

## settings
- Four themes plus your own: a `~/.themes/NAME/theme.conf` folder (colours, wallpaper, icons).
- "Dark from 19:00 to 07:00" switches by itself.

## files
- **Put Back** in the Trash returns items to where they came from.

## programs
- **Wine 10** runs Windows programs as a normal user: `sudo tpkg install wine`, then `tos-x wine program.exe`.

## developers
- The desktop's animations and screenshots are recorded by GitHub Actions (releases "animations" and "screenshots").
